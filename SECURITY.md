# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for security problems.

Report privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and click
**Report a vulnerability**. Include what you found, how to reproduce it and the
impact you expect. Reports in English or Portuguese are welcome.

You will get an acknowledgement within a few business days. Once the issue is
confirmed, a fix ships as soon as possible and the advisory is published after
users have had a chance to update. Credit is given unless you prefer otherwise.

## Scope

The code in this account's public repositories and the sites they deploy.
Out of scope: findings that need a compromised device or account, social
engineering, volumetric denial of service, and reports from automated scanners
without a demonstrated impact.

## Personal data

If a vulnerability exposes personal data, say so in the report. It is handled
as a priority, in line with Brazil's data protection law (LGPD).
